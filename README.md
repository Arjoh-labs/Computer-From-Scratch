# Computer-From-Scratch
Documenting my journey of understanding, designing, and building a computer from scratch, from transistors and logic gates to processors and complete computer systems.

**Building a Computer from Scratch**

This repository documents my journey toward understanding and building a computer from scratch. It is not just a hardware project; it is also my personal learning path into computer science, computer architecture, digital electronics, and low-level programming.

My goal is to gradually understand how computers work, starting from their fundamental building blocks and progressing toward more complex systems. Along the way, I will attempt to design and build my own computer while studying the concepts behind every component.

I will use this repository as a learning journal and project portfolio, documenting:
- My Learning Progress: Concepts, theories, and technical knowledge I acquire throughout the journey.
- Projects and Experiments: Practical implementations, prototypes, and experiments related to computer hardware and software.
- Research and Documentation: My understanding of digital logic, logic gates, transistors, processors, memory, and computer architecture.
- Challenges and Solutions: Problems I encounter, how I investigate them, and what I learn from solving them.
- Reflections: Insights, discoveries, mistakes, and improvements throughout the process.
I don't expect to understand everything immediately. This journey is about learning through experimentation, making mistakes, solving problems, and continuously improving.

My ultimate goal is to understand how a computer works from the ground up, not simply how to program one, but how to build the system itself.
